# A&M Peers, Historical Analogs and Forecast Snapshots

Peer cohorts are candidate comparison groups formed from PIT-safe properties such as national strength, roster/talent state, resources, regime transition and competition context. They may not be cherry-picked using the target game's outcome.

Historical analogs are retrieval/explanation candidates using strict-prior team, matchup, availability, regime and environment state. Analog similarity does not automatically alter a forecast.

Frequent A&M snapshots are immutable. Sunday/Monday/Wednesday/Friday/final-pregame and material-event refreshes are **cadence candidates**, not a frozen operational schedule. An earlier forecast is never overwritten by a later update.
