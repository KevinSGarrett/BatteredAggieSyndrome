# BAS Cross-Fit Labeling and Anti-Circularity

A valid historical expectation is produced before kickoff; trained only on information available before its historical training cutoff; excludes the target canonical game and mirrored rows; and excludes future games. Cross-fitting does **not** authorize training on future seasons merely because the target row is held out.

The primary BAS label uses a **BAS-independent national pregame expectation lane**. It may use normal PIT-safe football evidence but cannot consume the BAS label it creates or an A&M-underperformance target designed to encode away the effect being tested.

W14 specialization is a forecast challenger, not BAS ground truth. Peer cohorts and regime boundaries are frozen before protected outcomes are inspected. Market/other sensitivity anchors remain separate label versions.
