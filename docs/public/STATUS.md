# Status and limitations

**As of September 2, 2026: research preview; scientific validation incomplete.**

The repository contains data-processing code, forecast experiments, scoring functions, and validation tools. An end-to-end scientific audit is ongoing. Existing fitted forecast outputs are experimental and untrusted; they are not recommended predictions.

## Available for inspection

- Source code and small fixtures for research and software review.
- Implementations of national modeling, point-in-time data handling, and prospective evaluation concepts.
- Synthetic examples of scoring and consistency checks.
- A public explanation of the research question and intended methodology.

## Not established

- A validated production model or a recommended current-game forecast.
- A calibrated, publishable pregame BAS score.
- A persistent Texas A&M-specific underperformance effect.
- Causal claims about coaching, player availability, or program characteristics.
- A validated independent final-score prediction or deployed prediction service.
- Complete national feature coverage or universal historical pregame timestamp authority.

## Known limitations

Scientific review has identified problems in experimental probability/interval coherence, historical-to-current feature binding, and evidence timing. Corrections and software checks do not automatically validate all downstream scientific claims. Earlier outputs must not be presented as corrected results without explicit successor evidence.

Some research inputs are stale, missing, or not demonstrably available at the required historical cutoff. Unsupported cases must remain visible rather than being filled with fabricated defaults.

The 2024 and 2025 seasons have previously been exposed during project work. They cannot honestly be described as unseen holdout seasons. A replacement prospective evaluation protocol is not yet active.

External research data and access credentials are not included in the quick start. A passing small offline test does not establish a passing full-system run, prediction accuracy, or source-data completeness.

## What would change this status?

Independently reconstructible source evidence and metrics; correct current-opponent and temporal feature binding; coherent predictive distributions; properly separated chronological evaluation; and documented prospective performance with uncertainty. Any future public release must state its exact validated scope rather than claiming the entire project is error-free.
