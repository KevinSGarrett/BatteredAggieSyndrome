# Game Mechanics Intelligence — W13

## Opportunity versus efficiency
Expected possessions/opportunities are modeled separately from scoring efficiency.

## Tempo
Tempo/pace may use prior drives, plays and time, with score-state-adjusted candidates. No W13 pace formula is production-selected.

## Field position and hidden yards
Drive starts, opponent drive starts, punting, kickoffs, turnovers and fourth-down field-position consequences remain componentized. A hidden-yards aggregate may only be created with explicit double-count guards.

## Special teams
Field goals, punts, kickoffs, kick returns and punt returns are separate candidate components. Weather interactions and specialist continuity are experimental.

## Score state / garbage time
W13 registers all-plays, competitive-state filter, leverage-weighted and stratified candidates. No garbage-time numeric threshold is frozen.

## Penalties and turnovers
Penalty type, side and leverage are preferred over raw counts. Turnover skill/luck decompositions remain experimental.
