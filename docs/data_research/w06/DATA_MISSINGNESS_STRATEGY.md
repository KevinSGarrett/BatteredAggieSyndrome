# Wave 06 Data Missingness Strategy

Missingness is evidence, not permission to invent a value.

## Required distinctions
- **NOT_APPLICABLE** — source/domain legitimately does not apply.
- **NOT_PUBLISHED_YET** — information may arrive later; do not treat as negative/healthy.
- **SOURCE_NOT_COVERED** — conference/provider/year/game outside coverage.
- **RETRIEVAL_FAILED** — expected evidence could not be acquired.
- **UNRESOLVED_ENTITY** — evidence exists but identity join is uncertain.
- **REDACTED_OR_RESTRICTED** — known source exists but legitimate access/redistribution is unavailable.
- **UNKNOWN** — state cannot be inferred.

## Availability
Absence from an official report may mean available only when the governing policy explicitly defines unlisted players that way and the correct report/version was captured. Outside a reporting policy's scope, missing means unknown.

## Weather
If no issued forecast run exists for a historical cutoff, never substitute the later observation. Fall back to an earlier valid model/archive or mark weather forecast state missing with widened uncertainty.

## Market
Pure-football forecasts remain valid without market data. The market-augmented lane is unavailable when no timestamp-safe market observation exists.

## Resources and private institutions
Do not impute private-school football spending from public-school averages. Proxy resource variables retain explicit proxy type and uncertainty.

## Lower divisions
Sparse opponents receive coarser hierarchical priors and wider uncertainty rather than fabricated FBS-like detail.
