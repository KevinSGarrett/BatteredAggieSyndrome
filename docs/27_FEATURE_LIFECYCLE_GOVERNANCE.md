# Feature Lifecycle Governance — Wave 10

The accepted states are **CORE, SUPPORTED, CONDITIONAL, EXPERIMENTAL, REJECTED, BANNED**.

### State meaning
- CORE: repeatedly supported and stable for a specific target/scope.
- SUPPORTED: approved by evidence but not necessarily universal/core.
- CONDITIONAL: approved only under explicit lane, subgroup, coverage or era conditions.
- EXPERIMENTAL: safe enough to test; not production-approved.
- REJECTED: tested and did not justify use under the recorded experiment/version.
- BANNED: structurally unsafe/prohibited, such as leakage/future information or unresolved rights—not merely weak prediction.

Lifecycle state is **target-specific**. The same derived feature may be SUPPORTED for margin, REJECTED for win probability and EXPERIMENTAL for BAS.

Transitions are never automatic. Promotion requires recorded evidence references. BANNED cannot become usable through good model performance; its underlying temporal/rights/semantic classification must first be corrected through governance.

A rejection is retained. Reopening creates a new experiment/version rather than deleting the earlier failure.

### W10 baseline
All {736} handoff-permitted W09 raw-field seeds begin EXPERIMENTAL. There are **zero empirical promotions** in W10 because the required real historical matrices/walk-forward evidence are not yet available.
