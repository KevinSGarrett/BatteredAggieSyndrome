# DO NOT DRIFT

- Do not turn this into an A&M-only training dataset.
- Do not turn BAS into `P(A&M loses)` or a hand-weighted fan narrative.
- Do not use postgame/future/closing/realized evidence before its historical known-at time.
- Do not resurrect the unverified pretrained `.joblib` dependency.
- Do not make PFF, a cloud GPU, Kubernetes, microservices, a vector DB, or any fashionable tool mandatory without evidence.
- Do not feed ~900 raw source fields directly into production models without grain/PIT/feature governance.
- Do not let old A&M seasons masquerade as current-team state.
- Do not treat all FCS teams as identical, and do not recursively model every lower division at full resolution.
- Do not model injuries as simple starter counts; represent player/replacement/usage/uncertainty.
- Do not model coaches as one static rating or resources as a raw-dollar bonus.
- Do not interpret reconnaissance samples as population statistics or the pack as the full data lake.
- Do not let autonomous research modify the tests used to judge itself.
- Do not overwrite prior market/weather/availability/prediction snapshots.
- Do not confuse code completion with validated forecast quality.
- Do not bypass PIT state by letting features query mutable current/provider state directly.
- Do not make the research plane or an LLM a required dependency of deterministic pregame forecasts.
- Do not let dashboard/API serving silently recompute mutable forecasts instead of reading immutable forecast snapshots.
- Do not create a separate A&M canonical truth; high-resolution A&M evidence maps into the same canonical/PIT system.
- Do not treat W03's national→A&M interface as proof that a residual adapter, multi-task model, or any specific statistical form has already won.
- Do not add PostgreSQL, Kafka, Redis, an online feature store, Kubernetes or microservices merely because the architecture now has logical component boundaries.
- Do not allow future live/in-game observations into the same game's pregame feature path.

## W04 acceptance anti-drift
- Do not conflate Level-A/B/C class with PASS/FAIL evidence state.
- Do not silently turn Level-C hypotheses into release-blocking requirements.
- Do not invent numerical thresholds before their owner wave has evidence.
- Do not waive PIT/leakage/split/BAS cross-fit failures.
- Do not reintroduce licensing, terms, redistribution, scraping, provider-preference, or upstream-authorization gates for private local acquisition or training. Preserve that information as metadata and reserve review for an actual future distribution proposal.
- When an ADR changes a material boundary, update acceptance mappings in the same change.

## W08 PIT anti-drift
- Do not treat `published_at`, `effective_at`, `observed_at`, `retrieved_at` or a season label as interchangeable.
- Do not treat derived `prediction_eligible_at` as raw source truth or the only selection check.
- Do not replace historical issued weather forecasts with realized observations.
- Do not infer a player's health/availability because an official report is absent or the game was outside report-policy scope.
- Do not use a later corrected roster/report/line/source value when replaying an earlier cutoff.
- W08 synthetic tests are contract evidence, not proof that future materialized data are leakage-free.


## W10 feature anti-drift
- Do not call W10 candidate seeds CORE/SUPPORTED because they passed synthetic code tests.
- Do not hard-code a universal rolling window/EWMA decay from football intuition.
- Do not promote from correlation, mutual information, model importance, regularization or permutation alone.
- Do not use market-derived features in the pure-football lane.
- Do not erase REJECTED/BANNED/demotion history.
- Do not let BANNED future/leakage fields re-enter via strong model scores. A field may be banned for validity, privacy, malware, credential exposure, or schema incompatibility, but not solely for private-use licensing uncertainty.


## W12 player-intelligence anti-drift
- Do not equate roster membership, depth role, availability and actual participation.
- Do not backfill pregame depth/availability from later same-game snaps or outcomes.
- Do not infer healthy/available from a missing or noncovered official report.
- Do not convert player/position/replacement value to fixed game-point penalties without protected evidence.
- Do not stack injury count, starter-out and replacement-gap encodings without incremental ablation evidence.
- Do not use fixed conference transfer penalties; preserve same-player identity and continuous source/destination context.
- Do not route zero-college-snap freshmen through transfer-production translation.
- Do not use eventual NFL Draft outcomes as earlier historical roster features.
- Do not treat W12 source-lane plans as proof that historical reports/data have been materialized.
## W14 additions

- Do not force a nonzero A&M adjustment because A&M is the primary product target.
- Do not hard-code a Kyle Field/12th Man or fan-narrative bonus.
- Do not choose a specialization winner, peer/analog method, snapshot cadence or `THR-005` numeric value in W14.
