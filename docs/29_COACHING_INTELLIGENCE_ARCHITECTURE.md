# Coaching Intelligence Architecture — W13

## Status
Contract/reference architecture only. No calibrated coach effect is selected.

## Role episodes
A coach is represented by effective-dated role and responsibility episodes rather than one static rating. Formal title, unit responsibility, coordinator responsibility and play-caller responsibility are distinct evidence dimensions.

Promotions preserve prior role evidence as career context but do not add prior QB/WR/OC/HC effects as independent bonuses.

## Performance versus expectation
Candidate coach contribution is modeled as residual performance relative to an expectation that controls, where practical, for player/roster value, supporting cast, co-staff, scheme, injuries, continuity, opponent strength, schedule and game state.

Low-sample role effects should use hierarchical/partial pooling or an empirically superior regularized alternative.

## Decision evidence
Fourth-down, clock-management and halftime-adjustment metrics are experimental candidates. W13 freezes no numeric coach bonus and no production coach representation.

## W12/W11 boundary
Player value and availability remain owned by W12. Team prior/underlying/available/form semantics remain owned by W11. W13 coaching evidence feeds those interfaces without redefining them.
