# Research Adoption, Challenger Registration, and Promotion Bridge

> **Wave 18 full-rebuild status:** reference implementation + governance contract. No protected model result or production promotion is claimed.

## Research adoption

Adoption means the experiment deserves to exist as a tracked challenger for external evaluation. It does not mean it replaces the champion.

## Prerequisites

Research adoption requires compatible development evidence, replay verification, complete artifact manifest, valid W17 judging seal, no critical governance warnings, and a disposition allowed by the research plane.

## One-way promotion bridge

The W18 research system may produce `PROMOTION_REVIEW_REQUIRED`. A separate W17/W21 promotion evaluator later resolves protected evidence and champion/challenger state. Protected metrics may flow into that promotion review but cannot flow back into HPO/tournament search.

## Feature adoption

A feature-family challenger remains EXPERIMENTAL until lifecycle governance approves a state change. Tournament ranking alone cannot set CORE/SUPPORTED.

## Model adoption

A model challenger remains non-champion. A&M specialization retains the no-adjustment comparator. Pure-football remains present even when market augmentation is researched.
