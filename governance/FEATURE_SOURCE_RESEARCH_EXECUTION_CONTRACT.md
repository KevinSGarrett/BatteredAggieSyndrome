## Execution lanes

The program runs three safe parallel lanes:

1. Historical evidence backbone: national games, schedules, outcomes, canonical identities, official gamebooks, drives, plays, boxes, rosters, participants, talent, returning production, weather, availability, rankings, venues, and advanced statistics. Each season and domain has independent eligibility.
2. Candidate feature engineering: returning production/shared snaps; possession/pace/drive duration/workload; issued and observed weather; opponent-adjusted form and schedule stress; injury severity and return-to-performance; sustainability/luck; BAS volatility, downside tails, and collapse taxonomy.
3. Dependency-gated research: MAPIE after calibrated chronological baselines; bounded Optuna after stable deterministic pipelines and simple baselines; SHAP only for competitive tree challengers; PyMC/ArviZ only after deterministic residuals and simpler shrinkage models stabilize.

One incomplete source or domain must not block unrelated eligible work. Outcome-only seasons may support Elo and long-run team context without gaining roster, play, injury, weather, or advanced-stat authority.

## Data and source rules

Bulk raw, canonical, quarantine, feature, PIT, training, model, forecast, and manifest payloads remain under `C:\BatteredAggieSyndrome.data`, outside Git. Captures preserve source URL, endpoint/route, acquisition time, schema, content hash, immutable identity, reconciliation state, and known-at eligibility.

The approved source strategy is:

- mine pinned `cfbfastR-cfb-data` and `cfbfastR-raw` for historical evidence and transform validation;
- isolate `sportsdataverse-py` behind an adapter and never treat it as canonical truth;
- use the official `cfbd-python` client behind the source-adapter boundary;
- use official NCAA and school/conference archives for gamebook-equivalent evidence;
- use NOAA archived forecast products for issued forecasts and ISD/ASOS for observations;
- label ERA5/Open-Meteo reanalysis or enrichment explicitly;
- use Wayback with immutable original URL, capture time, and provenance identity;
- use nflverse and StatsBomb AMF only as reference/sandbox surfaces.

No name-only merge, invented contest ID, fabricated timestamp, guessed fact, or unproved zero is allowed. Missing evidence stays missing, is quarantined, or is imputed by an explicit versioned statistical rule.

## Feature and evaluation contract

Every candidate feature must define identity, population, grain, inputs, exact formula, known-at rule, missingness, imputation, structural-zero semantics, valid seasons/rule eras, source coverage, deterministic tests, empirical hypothesis, simpler reference, chronological/common-support comparison, artifacts, provenance, cleanup, and adoption decision.

It reports coverage and performance, including applicable win-probability, margin, score-distribution, calibration, interval, A&M-versus-national, season, venue, weather, favorite/underdog, and rule-era slices. Promotion requires stable material out-of-sample value, leakage safety, operational benefit, and an ablation beating simpler alternatives. Negative results are retained.

Protected claims require a sealed, genuinely untouched forecast-first 2026+ population or another immutable population proven not to have influenced development.
