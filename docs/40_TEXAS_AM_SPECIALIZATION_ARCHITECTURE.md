# Texas A&M Specialization Architecture — W14

## Purpose
Texas A&M is the primary product and evaluation target, but it does **not** receive a separate canonical truth or an A&M-only historical training universe. The national college-football foundation remains the statistical backbone. W14 freezes how higher-resolution A&M evidence can be layered on top of that foundation.

## Protected structure
1. Reuse W07 canonical identities and W08 PIT state.
2. Reuse W10 feature lifecycle, W11 team state, W12 player/availability state and W13 coaching/context/mechanics state.
3. Resolve A&M evidence at higher fidelity where legitimate sources exist.
4. Produce a versioned `TamuStateOverlay` with explicit uncertainty and provenance.
5. Feed national forecast + A&M overlay into **candidate** specialization mechanisms.
6. Always retain the unchanged national forecast as `TAMU-SP-00` no-adjustment baseline.
7. W16/W17 protected evidence may promote, shrink or reject nonzero specialization.

## What W14 does not claim
W14 does not prove that A&M needs a nonzero residual correction. It does not choose a model family, set `THR-005`, assign a Kyle Field/12th Man bonus, encode BAS fan narratives, or create production model performance.
