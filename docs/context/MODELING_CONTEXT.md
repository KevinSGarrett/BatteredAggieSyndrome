# Modeling Context

Core objective is probabilistic structured forecasting with strong simple baselines and empirical promotion. W03 defines three semantic model layers: national foundation, A&M specialization, and coherent forecast assembly/calibration/uncertainty.

The national→A&M interface is now explicit, but its statistical implementation is intentionally not frozen. Residual adapters, hierarchical multi-task approaches, mixture-of-experts or another method may win later evidence. A&M specialization is allowed to shrink to no adjustment.

Win, score, margin and BAS-related outputs must be composed through a coherent forecast-distribution boundary. Training and inference share PIT-safe feature contracts. Advanced neural/sequence/graph approaches remain challengers, not architecture assumptions.

## W11 team-state context
- Team state now separates prior, underlying, available and recent-form evidence.
- Early-season prior components and blending schedules remain experimental; no manual weights are frozen.
- Historical current-team relevance may depend on recency and effective-dated regime similarity, distinct from national training usefulness.
- FCS is a strong secondary level; lower divisions become progressively coarse and always carry translation uncertainty.
- No empirical team-state winner or cross-division parameter was selected in W11.
## W14 modeling context
A&M specialization candidate set is frozen for later W16/W17 comparison: no-adjustment national baseline, residual adapter, hierarchical/multi-task, partial pooling/shrinkage, calibration-only and optional stacked/mixture challenger. W14 selects none.


## W16 model architecture
W16 freezes a coherent joint score distribution as the preferred parent/reconciliation object for score, margin and win outputs; mandatory simple baselines; unselected advanced model/joint-score candidates; scenario simulation contracts; uncertainty/OOD/disagreement candidates; and pure-football vs market-augmented lane isolation. No trained metrics or model winner exist.

## W17 protected validation context
W17 freezes the judging protocol before protected model results. Development/tuning uses eligible history through 2023; 2024-2025 is the governance-protected holdout and 2026+ is forward shadow. Brier is primary for win probabilities, log loss/calibration are required, and score/margin/distribution metrics are predeclared. No trained model winner or protected score exists in W17.
