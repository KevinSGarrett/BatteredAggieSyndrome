# Effective-Dated Regulatory Environment — W13

The former `rule era` concept is expanded into an effective-dated regulatory environment.

Families include:
- playing rules;
- roster rules;
- eligibility;
- transfer environment;
- compensation/regulatory context when legitimately relevant.

Every record separates when the rule was announced from when it became effective. A rule may be known before it applies; conversely, later retrospective summaries cannot establish earlier knowledge.

Material rule changes version affected feature definitions. `season` alone is not sufficient when clock, overtime, targeting, penalties, roster, availability or transfer semantics change.
