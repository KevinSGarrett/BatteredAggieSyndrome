# Early-Season Priors and Blending — Wave 11

## Why priors matter
Before Game 1, current-season EPA, success rate and similar direct performance evidence do not exist. The state therefore needs a prior.

Candidate prior inputs include prior performance, returning production, recruiting, transfers, experience, QB state, coaching/scheme continuity, development history, program resources and division strength. Inputs remain subject to their owning waves and PIT availability.

## Resources are upstream
Program resources are candidate **preseason/program-capacity** information. They are not a weekly points bonus. As direct current-season evidence accumulates, direct evidence should be allowed to dominate if chronological validation supports that behavior.

## Blend candidates
W11 preserves several candidates:
- prior-only baseline;
- games-observed blend;
- pseudo-count update;
- precision/uncertainty-weighted blend;
- regime-aware dynamic blend.

No fixed Week-1/Week-2/Week-3 weight schedule is frozen.

## Selection
Selection must use chronological seasons, with explicit early-season slices, and must compare calibration as well as predictive error. A parameter that helps later-season forecasts but harms Weeks 1–3 is not automatically acceptable.

## Market lane
Preseason market information, if evaluated later, remains optional and market-augmented only. Pure-football priors must remain independently viable.
