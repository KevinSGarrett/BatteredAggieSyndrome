# Raw Field → Feature-Candidate Handoff — Wave 09

Wave 09 ends at **candidate-input eligibility**, not feature adoption.

A raw field may be handed to Wave 10 experiment design only when its W09 handoff state permits it and all downstream use passes the W08 PIT gateway. Metadata/join fields remain structural; banned/review-required fields do not enter candidate experiments until resolved. Historical-game outputs require prior-game aggregation. Effective-dated and as-of sources require snapshot reconstruction.

Wave 10 still must perform transformation design, missingness/data-quality checks, redundancy analysis, statistical screening, ablation, chronological validation and lifecycle promotion/demotion. No W09 label such as `W10_CANDIDATE_INPUT_ONLY` means CORE/SUPPORTED/production.
