# National Foundation ↔ Texas A&M Specialization Interface

## Protected objective

National data teaches the general game. Texas A&M receives deeper reconstruction and specialized forecasting treatment without turning the training universe into an A&M-only dataset.

## Conceptual input contract to A&M specialization

- canonical game identity;
- prediction timestamp / PIT context;
- national team/opponent strength distributions;
- national matchup representation;
- national baseline score/margin/win distribution;
- A&M high-resolution team/player/availability/staff/context state;
- source/data quality indicators;
- uncertainty.

## Conceptual output contract

The specialization layer returns an adjustment/representation plus uncertainty and lineage. It must be composable with the national forecast rather than silently replacing canonical national inputs.

## Anti-overfit rule

The architecture must permit partial pooling/shrinkage and a "no useful A&M-specific adjustment" outcome. A&M specialization is a product/evaluation priority, not permission to force a statistically unsupported Aggie effect.

## No duplicate truth

A&M official evidence can outrank national sources for A&M-specific factual observations under source-precedence rules, but it still maps into the same canonical identity/PIT system.
