# W11 Adaptive Review

## Questions and decisions
1. **Is W11 still the correct objective?** Yes. W10 left `TASK-030` ready and preserved all feature candidates as nonproduction.
2. **Should W11 pick a production recency/early-season formula now?** No. Real chronological matrices and protected model evaluation are not yet available.
3. **Should current-team relevance replace national historical training utility?** No. They are separate roles.
4. **Should staff/QB changes automatically reset history?** No. Use shrinkage/uncertainty candidates; hard reset requires evidence.
5. **Should lower divisions be modeled recursively at FBS depth?** No. Keep FCS strong and terminate with progressively coarse priors.
6. **Should W11 implement injury/replacement effects?** No. Freeze `available_strength` interface and leave player-specific construction to W12.
7. **Should W11 introduce a new deployable service/database?** No. Team state is a logical package inside the accepted modular monolith.
8. **Highest-value outcome:** a PIT-safe, uncertainty-aware state interface and experiment catalog that later waves can materialize without silently inheriting illustrative weights.
