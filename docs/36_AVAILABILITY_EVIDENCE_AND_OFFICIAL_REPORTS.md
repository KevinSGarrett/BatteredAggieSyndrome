# Availability Evidence & Official Report Lane — Wave 12

## Availability is broader than injury
The availability system includes injury, illness, suspension, disciplinary/academic absence, targeting carryover, personal absence, bowl opt-out, return-to-play limits, snap restrictions and unknown causes.

## Evidence confidence
W12 preserves evidence class, freshness, report version and policy scope. Official conference reports are the highest-confidence recent lane **when the report policy actually covers the game and report time**.

For Texas A&M, covered SEC availability reports are the highest-confidence recent official lane.

## Critical noncoverage rule
`no report` ≠ `healthy`.

If the relevant conference policy does not cover a game, a report version is missing, or the historical archive is incomplete, player availability remains UNKNOWN unless other PIT-eligible evidence supports a state.

## Revision rule
Later report versions do not rewrite earlier forecast snapshots. Each report/version is an immutable publication observation governed by W08 first-known/cutoff semantics.

## Materialization status
W12 defines the source lane and adapter/materialization plan. It does **not** claim the official report archive has been completely downloaded or reconstructed. Historical acquisition/materialization remains an implementation task for W19 with A&M/SEC priority.

## Sparse evidence
Weak/speculative reports may become probabilistic/uncertain evidence or remain unresolved. W12 assigns categorical authority tiers rather than fabricated numeric reliability weights.
