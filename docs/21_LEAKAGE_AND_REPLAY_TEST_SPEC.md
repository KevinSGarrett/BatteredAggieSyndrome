# Leakage and Replay Test Specification

W08 establishes a protected synthetic battery that must remain additive as new leakage classes are discovered.

The battery covers at minimum:
- later injury/availability revisions;
- market observations after an earlier forecast cutoff;
- realized/observed weather substituted for an issued forecast;
- weather runs unavailable at cutoff;
- rule publication vs target effective date;
- financial reporting-period vs public-release timing;
- same-game/postgame outputs;
- future NFL Draft/postseason outcomes;
- retrospective bios/current pages;
- future roster/depth states;
- source corrections published after the snapshot;
- prior completed-game aggregates that are legitimately eligible.

A synthetic PASS verifies contract behavior only. Production acceptance still requires source-contract integration tests and chronological historical replay after materialization.
