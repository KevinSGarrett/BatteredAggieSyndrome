# Preseason Honors & Draft Development — Wave 12

## Preseason honors
Preseason conference teams, recognized preseason All-America teams and official award/watch lists are candidate external-consensus priors.

They may encode scouting reputation, expected role and returning-player quality, but overlap with recruiting, prior production, draft reputation and team reputation.

Therefore:
- one row per honor observation;
- preserve selector/scope/team level/position/announced_at/source;
- usable only after `announced_at`;
- no manual first-team/second-team/national weighting is frozen;
- later feature-family ablation decides whether they help.

## NFL Draft information
A historical game's current roster cannot use knowledge of which players will eventually be drafted.

BANNED for an earlier game:
- eventual draft status;
- eventual round/pick;
- later combine/pro-day outcome;
- retrospective “future NFL player” label.

Legitimate candidates:
- completed prior program draft production;
- completed prior position-group development;
- lagged coach/player-development evidence;
- future draft outcome as a retrospective training target for a leakage-safe player-development model;
- reproducible timestamped preseason draft projections, only if source/history/rights permit.

No draft-development candidate is production-selected in W12.
